# ADR-015 Improvement round 1: one new ladder level "weather detail and weather memory" (change after design freeze F2)
status: accepted        phase: p3 ladder (re-run P3 to P6)        owner: chief
context: the analyst's residual audit of the Phase 3 target (reports/analysis/audit.md, EXP-A1..A3) found the error concentrated in working-day rush hours (20% of rows, 48% of squared error) and in weather situation 3 (7.8% of rows, 21.5%), and a diagnostic tree model 0.018 ahead on held-out days. A post-freeze change must beat the noise on held-out days and not lose on the chronological split.
options: stop at C5 / add the tested representation terms as one ladder level / also change the loss (weighted or Poisson fit: rejected, not one of the course algorithms' losses)
evidence: exp/analyst/hyp_combo.json, closed-form fits, deltas against C5 on seeded / held-out days / chronological: humidity powers, wind terms and workingday x hour x temp +0.0073 / +0.0101 (paired se 0.0011) / +0.0203; weather situation of the previous hours +0.0041 / +0.0107 (0.0019) / +0.0107; both +0.0080 [+0.0031, +0.0136] / +0.0169 (0.0025, 5 of 5 folds) / +0.0256. Rejected in the same audit: square-root trend, year step, hour x year, day type x time, free holiday profile (each loses on at least one validator).
decision: insert ladder level C6_weather_detail after C5 with blocks wx_detail (hum^2, hum^3, windspeed^2, temp x windspeed), wd_x_hr_x_temp, ws_memory (weather situation one hour earlier, and the worst situation of the previous three hours; inputs only, looked up by timestamp, own value when the earlier hour is missing). The Phase 3 rule (ADR-013) is unchanged and decides whether the level becomes the target. The old C6 keeps its two remaining blocks.
consequences: the ladder was extended after we had seen Phase 3 results and about 40 looks at the validation set, so its validation score is more optimistic than the others; the report says so and quotes held-out days and the chronological split beside it. Rows are no longer transformed one by one: the two memory columns need the neighbouring hours of the same file (for the hidden test file, the union of both files' input columns, no labels). This is the only improvement round we run: after it the held-out-day score is expected within 0.002 of the diagnostic ceiling (stop condition b).
